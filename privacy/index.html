Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026 · Last updated 5 November 2026

The Aletheia Institute builds private, self-hosted systems, and holds itself to the same standard it sells. This page says plainly what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and what we will never do with it.

1. Who we are

The Aletheia Institute ("we", "us") operates the website at aletheiainstitute.ai and the telephone and messaging services described below. Questions about this policy, or any request concerning your information, should go to inquiry@aletheiainstitute.ai.

2. The website collects nothing

This site makes no external requests. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no third-party scripts or fonts, and has no server that receives form data. The inquiry form composes a message in your own mail client; nothing is transmitted until you press send, and then it goes to us as ordinary email. You can verify this claim yourself — the site reports its own external-request count on every page load, and the source is published to be read.

3. What we collect when you call or message us

If you telephone us, or send us a text message, or we exchange messages at your request, we may collect and keep:

  • your mobile or telephone number;
  • your name and any contact details you choose to give us;
  • the content of your messages, and of your call where a call is transcribed;
  • a record of your consent to be messaged — what you agreed to, how, and when;
  • the date, time and duration of the contact.

Where a call is answered or transcribed by an automated system, you are told so at the start of the call, before anything is processed. If a call is recorded, you are told that too, and you may decline.

4. Why we hold it

To answer you, to carry out what you asked us for, to keep a record of consent as the law requires, and to meet our legal and accounting obligations. We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, and we do not use the content of your calls or messages to train machine-learning models.

5. Mobile information is never sold or shared

No mobile information will be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

The only parties who ever see a message are you, us, and the telecommunications carrier that must carry it to your handset. Carriers process message data solely to deliver it. We use no advertising networks, no data brokers and no analytics vendors.

6. Who else processes your information

We keep the number of parties as small as the work allows:

  • Our telecommunications carrier, to originate and deliver calls and messages. It receives your number and the message content because delivery is impossible without them.
  • Our own infrastructure, which we host and operate ourselves. Call audio, transcripts and message history are held on systems under our control, not on a third-party AI platform.

We do not sell personal information, and we have not sold or shared personal information in the preceding twelve months.

7. How long we keep it

Messages, transcripts and call records are kept for as long as needed to answer you and to meet our legal obligations, and then deleted. Consent and opt-out records are kept for as long as the law requires us to be able to prove them, which is longer than the messages themselves. You may ask us to delete anything we are not required to keep.

8. Your choices

  • Stop messages at any time by replying STOP. We will send one confirmation and nothing further.
  • Get help by replying HELP, or by writing to inquiry@aletheiainstitute.ai.
  • Ask what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to the same address. We will not charge you, and we will not treat you differently for asking.
  • Decline recording or automated handling at the start of a call, and ask for a person instead.

9. Children

Our services are for businesses and the adults who run them. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Information is held on infrastructure we operate ourselves, with access restricted to the people who need it. No system is perfect, and we do not claim otherwise; we design so that a failure exposes as little as possible.

11. Changes

If this policy changes we will post the new version here and change the date at the top. Material changes to how we handle mobile information will be described plainly rather than buried.

12. Contact

The Aletheia Institute — inquiry@aletheiainstitute.ai. We read everything that arrives there.

See also our Terms of Service, which carry the messaging programme terms.

This site calls no one. Neither will your data.
